headline: Credit scoring AI has no outside auditor, so the provider's own file has to hold up

If your bank builds a credit-scoring model in-house, no outside body will check it before launch. You do the conformity assessment yourself, and you must be able to prove it.

Credit scoring sits in Annex III point 5(b). Article 43(2) says high-risk systems in Annex III points 2 to 8 follow the internal-control procedure in Annex VI. That procedure does not involve a notified body, which is an outside conformity assessment body. Only Annex III point 1, biometrics, has an outside route.

Annex VI is short. It has three checks, and the provider does all of them. First, verify that the quality management system meets Article 17. Second, examine the technical documentation against the requirements in Chapter III, Section 2. Third, verify that design, development and post-market monitoring under Article 72 match that documentation.

Article 16 makes the provider finish this before the system is placed on the market or put into service. The provider then draws up the EU declaration of conformity and affixes the CE marking. Putting a model into service for your own use counts.

Article 43(4) asks for a new assessment after a substantial modification. For models that keep learning, there is one relief. Changes the provider pre-determined at the first assessment, and recorded in the technical documentation (Annex IV point 2(f)), are not a substantial modification. So write down your retraining limits and expected performance ranges at the start.

The Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, rewrote Article 43(3). It left Article 43(2) and Annex VI as they were. Annex III duties apply from 2 December 2027.

What to do now: build the Annex VI record early. Give each of the three checks a named owner, a date and saved evidence. If you buy the system instead, you are a deployer. A CE marking on it rests on the vendor's own assessment, not an independent audit. Ask for the declaration of conformity and the instructions for use, and keep your own Article 26 duties in view.

General information, not legal advice.
