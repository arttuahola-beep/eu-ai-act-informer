headline: Selling smart products? Article 24 can make you check the AI paperwork
source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Articles 6(1), 23, 24, 25 and 113 and Annex I; Digital Omnibus Regulation (EU) 2026/1744
---

If you sell products with AI built in, Article 24 of the AI Act can make you a distributor with checks to run before you list them. It only applies to high-risk AI. For products under Annex I, it applies from 2 August 2028, after the Digital Omnibus Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 moved the date.

When does a product count? Under Article 6(1), the AI must be a safety component of a product, or be the product itself. The product must fall under Union legislation listed in Annex I, such as machinery, toys or radio equipment. It must also need a third-party conformity assessment under that legislation. A smart toy with an AI safety function is one possible case. Most retail AI, such as chatbots and recommenders, is not.

What must a distributor check? Before making the system available, Article 24(1) asks you to verify four things. The system has the CE marking. It comes with the EU declaration of conformity. It comes with the instructions for use. The provider and importer have done their own duties.

What if something looks wrong? If you have reason to think the system does not conform, do not sell it until it is fixed (Article 24(2)). If it is already on sale, take corrective action, or withdraw or recall it (Article 24(4)). If it presents a risk, tell the provider or importer at once, and the authorities too if it is already on the market. Keep storage and transport from harming compliance (Article 24(3)). On a reasoned request, give the authority your documents and cooperate (Article 24(5)-(6)).

Your role can change. If you put your own brand on such a product, you become its provider (Article 25(1)(a)). If you import it under a non-EU brand, you are an importer under Article 23. Importers must keep the key documents for 10 years (Article 23(5)).

Best practice: add one question to supplier onboarding for own-label and imported goods. Does the product have an AI function that affects safety? If yes, collect the declaration of conformity and the instructions for use now, and record which role you hold.

General information, not legal advice.
