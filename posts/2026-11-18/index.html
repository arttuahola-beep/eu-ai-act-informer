Wednesday 18 November 2026 Financial institutions

Tell loan applicants when AI helps decide: CCD2 starts this Friday, the AI Act notice follows

If a model helps decide on a consumer loan, the applicant must be told. Two EU laws now say so. The first one starts in two days.

From 20 November 2026, national rules under the second Consumer Credit Directive (CCD2), Directive (EU) 2023/2225, apply (Art. 48(1)). Article 18(8) covers any creditworthiness assessment that involves automated processing of personal data. In that case the consumer can ask for human intervention.

That right has three parts. The consumer can get a clear explanation of the assessment, including its logic and risks (point (a)). They can give their own view (point (b)). They can ask for a review of the assessment and of the credit decision (point (c)). The creditor must tell the consumer that this right exists.

The trigger is broad. GDPR Article 22 covers only decisions based solely on automated processing. CCD2 Article 18(8) does not have that limit. So a model score that an underwriter signs off still counts.

Refusals add a duty. Under Article 18(9), a creditor that rejects an application must tell the consumer without delay. Where the assessment was based on automated processing, the creditor must also say so. It must explain the right to human assessment and how to contest the decision.

CCD2 is a directive, so it applies through national law. Transposition is uneven. In January 2026 the Commission sent formal notices to 23 Member States that had not fully transposed it. Check the law in each country where you lend.

The AI Act adds its own notice. Credit scoring of natural persons is high-risk under Annex III point 5(b) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Under Article 26(11), a deployer of an Annex III system that makes or helps make decisions about people must tell them that the system is being used on them. This applies from 2 December 2027, the Annex III date set by the Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. The Omnibus did not change Article 26(11).

Article 86 later gives a right to an explanation of the AI system's role in an individual decision. It applies only where other Union law does not already give that right (Art. 86(3)). For consumer credit, CCD2 may already cover much of it.

Practical step: build one applicant notice and one review route now. Use them for CCD2 this week. Extend them to meet Article 26(11) before December 2027. Log each request, what you explained and what the review found.
