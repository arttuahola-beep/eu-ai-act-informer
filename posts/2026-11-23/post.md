**Takeaway:** Your online shop may have outgrown SME size but still have fewer than 750 staff. If so, the Digital Omnibus gives you some AI Act relief. It lowers some fine caps and simplifies some paperwork. It does not touch the bans or the core duties.

**Who counts.** The Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, added a new category to the AI Act. This is the small mid-cap enterprise, or SMC (Art. 3(14b)). The definition comes from Commission Recommendation (EU) 2025/1099. An SMC is not an SME. It has fewer than 750 staff. Its turnover is at most €150 million, or its balance sheet is at most €129 million. SMEs are still defined by Recommendation 2003/361/EC (Art. 3(14a)).

**Fines.** For large firms, each fine cap is the higher of two figures: a fixed sum or a share of worldwide turnover. Art. 99(6a) reverses this for SMCs, so the lower figure applies. It covers two tiers. Art. 99(4) caps fines at €15 million or 3%. That tier includes deployer duties (Art. 26) and transparency duties (Art. 50). Art. 99(5) caps fines at €7.5 million or 1% for misleading information given to authorities. Example: an SMC with €120 million turnover faces a 99(4) cap of €3.6 million, not €15 million.

**What it leaves out.** Art. 99(6a) does not cover Art. 99(3). So breaking an Article 5 ban, such as using manipulative dark patterns, still risks up to €35 million or 7%, whichever is higher. SMEs get the lower figure in all three tiers (Art. 99(6)). SMCs do not.

**Paperwork.** Some retailers provide a high-risk system themselves, for example an in-house pay-later credit check (Annex III 5(b)). Art. 11(1) lets SMEs and SMCs prepare the Annex IV technical documentation in a simpler way. They do this on a form the Commission must create. Using it is optional. If you choose the simpler route, you must use that form. Art. 17(2) says quality management should match the provider's size, and it names SMCs. But the required rigour and level of protection do not change. The lighter quality-management route in Art. 63(1) stays open to SMEs only. These high-risk duties apply from 2 December 2027 (Art. 113, as amended by 2026/1744).

**Also useful.** When setting penalties, Member States must consider the interests and economic viability of SMEs and SMCs (Art. 99(1)).

**Best practice.** Check your size class now. Use the Recommendation's counting rules, which can include partner and linked companies. Record the result next to your AI inventory. Do not treat SMC status as a reason to ease off on Article 5 or Article 50.
